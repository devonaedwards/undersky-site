What is Co-Star good at?

Co-Star is the app that got a whole generation talking about their rising sign. It uses real astronomy data, the writing is blunt in a way nobody else has pulled off, and you can see how your chart lines up with your friends'.

If you want astrology to be something you share with people, Co-Star is still the one everyone else copies.

What is CHANI good at?

CHANI is one astrologer's voice, and that is the point of it. Chani Nicholas writes and records the guidance herself, and it is warm and openly political.

It is built for someone who wants to be walked through what is happening, not handed a chart and left to it. If you want a teacher, start here.

What is The Pattern good at?

The Pattern reads less like astrology and more like a description of you, which is why people find it a bit uncanny. It is good on relationships and on what phase of life you are in.

If you are here for understanding yourself rather than for the sky, this is the strongest one.

What is TimePassages good at?

TimePassages comes from a serious traditional background and is built for people who already know astrology. The charts are precise, the writing is sober, and it does not try to be a social app.

If you are studying astrology rather than just reading it, this is the proper tool.

What is Sanctuary good at?

Sanctuary connects you to a real astrologer, live. That is a different thing from everything else here, and for some questions it is the right thing, because a person can hear what you are actually asking.

Undersky shows its working, keeps your birth details on your phone, and reads each relationship as what it actually is. We make it, so read this section with that in mind, and check the claims.

Common questions

Which astrology app is best for learning astrology?

TimePassages if you want professional chart tools. Undersky if you want the exact degrees kept in view, a choice between whole sign and Placidus houses, and an explanation of what each reading was built from.

Are there astrology apps without a subscription?

Most of the well-known ones are subscriptions. Undersky is not. Your daily horoscope, your birth chart and its reading, and your year overview are free, and anything extra is a one-time purchase.

Can an astrology app tell you why it said what it said?

Most will not. Undersky, and its sibling apps Muhura for Vedic astrology and Gilsung for Four Pillars, all have a "Show what this reading came from" button that lists what the reading was built from.

Does an astrology app need your exact birth time?

It needs it for your rising sign and your houses, which change every few minutes. It does not need it for your sun sign or most planet positions. An app that works things out on your phone can use your exact time without that time going anywhere.
