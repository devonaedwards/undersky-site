What is synastry?

Synastry is comparing two whole birth charts to each other, rather than comparing two sun signs. It looks at where one person's planets land on the other person's chart.

That is why it can say something about one specific relationship, instead of something general about Leos and Scorpios. It also works for any two people, not just couples. That last part is what most apps ignore.

Why does the type of relationship matter?

Because the same two charts mean different things depending on who the two people are. A tense aspect between two charts is one thing in a marriage and quite another between a mother and a nine-year-old.

Common questions

What is the difference between synastry and a composite chart?

Synastry compares two charts to each other, planet by planet. A composite chart merges the two into one chart that stands for the relationship itself. Synastry describes how you meet. A composite describes what you make together.

Is astrology compatibility accurate for friends and family?

The chart comparison works for any two people. What has to change is the reading itself, because advice written for lovers reads wrongly for a sibling or a child. Look for an app that asks what the relationship is.

Do both people need the app for compatibility?
