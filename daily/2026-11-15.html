What is a first quarter moon?

A first quarter moon is the half lit Moon that stands ninety degrees from the Sun, a week or so after the new moon, and it is a square between them.

Half the disc is lit and it rises around midday and sets around midnight, so it is the obvious evening moon. Illumination sits near fifty percent and is rising.

Because the Sun and Moon are square, this phase reads as the point where a beginning meets its first real friction: the cost becomes visible, and something has to be adjusted or committed to. That tension is where the growth in the cycle actually lives.

Nothing about a square decides anything for you. It marks the place in the month where a decision is worth making deliberately rather than by default.
