Why does the sky repeat but never exactly?

Every body in the chart runs on its own cycle length, so the pattern of the whole sky recurs in pieces but the full combination almost never comes back.

The Moon returns to a sign every twenty seven days or so, the Sun every year, Mars every two years, Jupiter every twelve, Saturn every twenty nine and a half. Any two of those line up fairly often. All of them at once effectively do not.

That is what makes a daily sky worth reading more than once. The Moon in Scorpio comes round thirteen times a year, but the Moon in Scorpio with Venus opposite Saturn and Mercury retrograde is a specific configuration that may not recur in your lifetime.

It is also why astrology is built out of cycles rather than one off events. The recurring parts give you something to compare against, and the combinations keep it from being the same reading twice.
