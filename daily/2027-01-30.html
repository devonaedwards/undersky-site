How do you read a daily sky page?

A daily sky page is a short list of where the visible bodies actually are, ordered from the slowest background to the fastest detail.

Start with the Sun's sign, which is the month you are in. Then the Moon's sign and phase, which is the part that changes daily. Ingresses tell you a body crossed into a new sign today, stations tell you one paused to change direction, and exact aspects list the angles that came exact within the day.

Every one of those is a measured position, computed for the tropical zodiac at a fixed moment in universal time. None of it depends on where you live, and none of it is a forecast about you specifically.

The reading is the part you add. A sky is a set of themes that are available today, and what you decide to do with any of them stays entirely with you.
