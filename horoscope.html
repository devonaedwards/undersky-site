What is an opposition in astrology?

An opposition is a one hundred and eighty degree angle, two bodies facing each other across the chart, and it reads as two true things pulling in different directions.

Signs six apart share a mode and sit in complementary elements, so an opposition is a real polarity rather than a mismatch. A full moon is the most familiar one: the Sun and Moon opposite each other, both fully in play.

Because an opposition often shows up through other people, it is easy to experience it as someone else being difficult. The more useful frame is that the far end of the axis is a part of you that is currently being carried by somebody else.

The point is not to pick a side and call it settled. It is to see both ends clearly and decide what balance you actually want, which is a choice you get to make.
