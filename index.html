Built for the people you love

Every relationship, its own lens

Partner, parent, child, sibling, friend, coworker. Undersky is the one astrology app where a parent and child chart never reads like a date, and readings about your kids stay developmental, always, enforced by the app itself.

Real astronomy underneath

Positions computed with precise astronomy and checked against independent sources. Eclipses, retrogrades, and returns come from the real sky, never guessed. The exact degrees are always there in Explore when you want them.

A sky that surprises you

Your daily and weekly readings know when an eclipse is coming, when a planet turns, and when your Saturn return begins, because the app computes the actual sky. Quiet days are honest too.

Your data is yours. Full stop.

Your birth details, your charts, and your readings stay on your phone, and we never collect them. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," because there is nothing for us to collect. Honestly, we think it is weird and gross that other companies harvest this and do who knows what with it. Astrology is personal. We just built an app that treats it that way, by design, from the very first screen.

Free every day. The rest, only if you want it.

Your horoscope is free, every day, forever. Your natal chart and its reading are free. The overview of your year is free. When you want more, it stays simple: relationship readings are a small one-time purchase that is yours to keep, and grows as your relationship does, however many times around the sun it lasts. Precise forecasts, timed to the minute, are there when you want them for planning. No subscriptions, no renewals, nothing to cancel.
