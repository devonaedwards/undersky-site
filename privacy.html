Privacy policy

Undersky is published by Pacefyx, Inc. Privacy contact: hello@undersky.app. Last updated July 2026.

The short version

Undersky has no developer server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracker. It makes no network call to a model or to us. Your birth details, charts, people, readings, and conversations are created and kept on your phone, and we never collect them. Because there is no copy on our side, we cannot read, search, or delete your content. You control the parts that can hold a copy: your own device backup, the app stores, a support email you choose to send, and anything you decide to share.

What Undersky is

Undersky calculates and displays astrology content: charts, readings, forecasts, and relationship comparisons. It is for reflection and entertainment. It is not professional medical, mental-wellbeing, financial, legal, or emergency advice. Astrology is not a factual prediction.

What the app processes, and where

The app processes only what you choose to enter or create. The exact set depends on the features you use. All of it is processed on your device.

WhatExamples
Birth and place detailsBirth date, time, and place; a place you select; your current place when you use location features.
People and relationshipsOptional names, saved people, relationship labels, and chart inputs from an invite.
Readings you keepForecasts, chart interpretations, saved readings, and what you ask the app to remember, all held locally.
Keepsakes and sharingKeepsake and share-card text or images you create. A copy leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it.
Settings and local recordsPreferences, a legal acknowledgement, reminder choices, and local purchase or entitlement state.

Some relationship details can reveal sensitive subjects about you or another person. Please do not enter anything you would not want kept on your phone, included in a backup, or deliberately shared. An adult who adds a family member, including a child, is responsible for having the right to do so.

Where the processing happens

The app calculates charts, stores your content, runs its safety checks, and composes your readings on your device. There is no developer account, developer server, cloud model, analytics, advertising, tracker, or network model call.

This is a statement about what the developer collects. It does not mean a copy cannot exist elsewhere: a device backup you keep, an operating-system service, an app-store transaction, or a person you share with can each hold a copy. Those are described below.

What we receive

For the content described above, we receive nothing. We hold no server copy, account record, analytics profile, advertising profile, or model transcript.

Support is a separate, voluntary path. Settings opens an email to hello@undersky.app rather than an in-app form. If you email us, your mail provider gives us your address and whatever you choose to include. The app does not attach your chart or readings to that email.

The app stores

Apple, and Google where Google Play is offered, run their own store, payment, account, and support systems. They may process store-account, purchase, receipt, billing, tax, device, fraud, and refund information under their own terms and privacy notices. We may receive limited transaction or entitlement information needed to make a purchase available in the app. We do not receive your birth, chart, reading, relationship, or keepsake content through the store. See Apple Privacy and Google Privacy. Use the store's own refund route for a store-billed purchase, and you keep any mandatory local consumer rights.

How your readings are made

Your readings are composed on your device from your own chart and a library of writing we prepared and reviewed in advance. Nothing is written while you use the app, and nothing you enter is sent to us or used to train anything. Readings are for reflection and entertainment, and are not a factual prediction or a professional medical, mental-wellbeing, financial, legal, or emergency service.

Keeping, deleting, backups, and sharing

Your content stays on your phone until you edit or delete it, use "Start over" or another local deletion control, or delete the app. Those controls remove the app's content and its local derived copies. Local purchase records may be kept so the app can recognize what you own; store purchase records stay with the store.

If you use a device backup, your content may be included in it. A backup is controlled by you and your operating-system or backup provider, not by us. It can still hold a copy after the app is deleted, and restoring it can restore your data. We cannot search, change, or erase your backup. To remove data from a backup, manage that backup with your device or backup provider.

Anything you share (a share card, keepsake, export, or invite) is user directed. Once you or a recipient shares it elsewhere, we cannot delete that copy. Please share only with people you trust.

Age

Undersky is for people 16 or older. An adult may enter information about family members, including children, for a relationship reading. Please do not enter a child's information unless you are authorized to do so.

Your rights, and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to, or receive a copy of your information, to withdraw consent where consent is the basis, and to complain to a regulator. Because Undersky has no developer backend, the primary way to exercise these rights over app content is on your device:

  • View or edit your profile, places, people, readings, and keepsakes in the app.
  • Use "Start over" or the applicable local delete control to remove the app's content.
  • Delete the app, and separately manage any device or backup copies you no longer want.
  • Use the store's account, purchase, receipt, and refund controls for anything held by Apple or Google.

You may also contact hello@undersky.app. We will tell you whether we hold the information in your request. If we hold no copy, we will say so and point you to the on-device, store, mail, or backup control that can act on it.

Your region

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. You have the data-protection rights described above.   • Other regions. Where local law provides additional rights or a complaint authority, the market version of this policy names or links to it. You can complain to your local authority even though we hold no copy of your app content.

Contact and emergencies

For a privacy question, email hello@undersky.app. Please do not use this address to report an immediate emergency. The app does not monitor users or contact emergency services for them. If someone is in immediate danger, use your local emergency number.
